# D10 — closure complement

**This file adds nothing to the wave and changes nothing in it.** It is a read-only
re-reading of the two D10 runs that are already reported in
`experiments/d10/results.md` (replay, `d10-001`) and
`experiments/d10/results_live.md` (live, `d10-live-001`), of the two datasets under
`data/pons/`, and of `experiments/d10/rpc_ledger.json`. No run, parameter, config,
checkpoint or dataset was touched; no neural round was started; no RPC call was
made. Every number below comes from a committed log record — cited by its `kind`
and its `episode_id` or `cutoff_ts` — or from a dataset file. Where a number
cannot be obtained that way it says **not computable from the logs** and why
(section E).

**Replay and live are kept strictly apart** and are never pooled, exactly as the two
reports keep them apart. The frozen branch is marked as the frozen control
throughout; it is a mechanical control, not a scientific comparison.

**Recomputation.** The leg-level wei numbers are not in the records — `Fill.as_dict`
rounds a price to eight decimals, and a curve price near 1.7e-09 rounds to `0.0`
there — so they were re-derived by `experiments/d10/closure_complement.py`, which
imports `flytrade.pons.curve` unchanged, anchors on the reconstructed curve state
each `ROUND` record already publishes for the chosen candidate at its cutoff,
advances it through the dataset's own events to the fill block and the exit block,
and asserts that the re-quoted `tokens_out` equals the `tokens_out_wei` in the
`EXECUTION` record. **That assertion passes for all seventeen entries** (7 + 8
replay, 2 live), so the legs printed here are the run's own legs, read at full
precision. The encoder drive in B.2 is likewise recomputed from `config.json`'s
declared channel map and its three declared constants and checked against a logged
`DECISION.stimulus.rates_hz` — the largest difference is 3.9e-05 Hz, which is the
log's own 4-decimal rounding — so no connectome is loaded anywhere in this file.

## A. Per-episode table

Seventeen entries in all: **7 settled** in `d10-001/learning`, **8 settled** in
`d10-001/frozen_reference` (the frozen control), **1 settled and 1
`PENDING_CONFIRMATION`** in `d10-live-001`. The frozen branch shares its first two
episodes with the learning branch and diverges from the third onward; that is a
fact about the two logs, not a comparison between them.

**How to read the columns.**

* *cutoff block (grid)* is the block the decision's cutoff timestamp maps to through
  the sparse header grid (one header per 100 blocks, reviewer decision 2); a `*`
  marks an interpolated answer, declared to ± 10.1 s. The `DECISION` record's own
  `block_number` field is **the token's launch block**, not a decision block
  (`PonsLoop._chain_extra`), and is reported separately in the table as *launch block*.
* *fill block* is the first block at or after `cutoff + 2 s` (`paper.fill_at`).
* *since last trade* is measured on the token's own event stream in the dataset:
  the cutoff minus the `block_timestamp` of the last `CurveBuy`/`CurveSell` at or
  before the cutoff.
* *external CurveBuy / CurveSell between fill and exit* counts the public trades with
  `fill_ts < block_timestamp <= exit_ts`; their *net quote flow* is `+` a buy's net
  into the curve and `−` a sell's gross out of it, the same signed quantity
  `TokenTape.apply` stores as `flow`.
* *net (recomputed)* is `quote_out − spent − gas` from the leg integers, printed
  beside the `net_pnl` the `OUTCOME` record carries. The two agree to the record's
  own 8-decimal rounding on every settled episode.
* *pre-clip amount* is `|net_pnl / notional| / 0.01` before the cap; *applied amount*
  is what the `LEARNING` record recorded.
* A `PENDING_CONFIRMATION` episode has **no exit leg**: nothing was quoted, nothing
  settled, nothing was taught. Its rows read `—` rather than a number.

### B.1 How the "26–35 s median" was computed, and what it means

**Where it came from.** It is `experiments/d10/dataset_report.md` §3, row *median age

**What it means.** The donor's collection plan is `sampleEndSeconds: 62`
(`dataset_report.md` §3): it captured the opening minute of each launch and stopped.

the maximum is 62 s in all four rows, and the p90 is 61–62 s. The 26 s and 35 s are

right-censored at 62 s and they are **not** lifetimes: they say nothing about whether
a token traded at 3 minutes or at 3 hours, because nothing after 62 s was collected.
That is the same fact that made this dataset unable to carry a 15-minute horizon and
that produced reviewer decision 1.

end"; the censored group is empty by the window's own design, and the 72 native

**How inactivity is represented in `pons_context_v1` / `pons_encoder_v1`.**

* `pons_context_v1` (`flytrade/pons/context.py`) treats a window with no trades as a
  **valid measurement**, not an absence: `trade_rate_2m = 0.0`, `flow_imb_2m = 0.0`
  (the module's own `total == 0` branch), `rv_2m = 0.0` (fewer than two per-trade
  steps), and `ret_30s` / `ret_2m` / `ret_5m` / `drawdown_5m` all `0.0` once the
  window's start price and the cutoff price are the same tape point — which is what
  happens as soon as the last trade falls out of the window, because the marginal
  price is a function of the reserves and the reserves have not moved. Only `age`
  keeps growing. *Absence* is a different thing and has its own vocabulary:
  age < 60 s or fewer than 3 trades makes the observation `INSUFFICIENT_TAPE` and it
  is **not presented at all**.
* `pons_encoder_v1` (`flytrade/pons/encoder.py` wrapping
  `flytrade.encoder.MarketToSensoryEncoder`) gives each feature an antagonistic pair
  of glomeruli, weight `C + (1−C)·max(0, ±u)` with carrier `C = 0.10`, then scales the
  16 channels to a constant 12,000 Hz total ORN drive over 935 ORNs. A feature at
  exactly 0 therefore puts **both** of its glomeruli on the carrier alone. A token
  that has been quiet long enough for every window to empty presents **fifteen
  channels on the carrier and one channel — `VL2a`, the positive half of `age` —
  carrying the rest of the budget.**

### B.3 How the paper sell treats the hypothetical buy's state

**The exact code path.** `flytrade/pons/paper.py`:

1. `PonsPaperExecution.open_long` → `plan_buy(tape, cutoff, clock)` → `fill_at(tape,
   cutoff + 2, clock)` returns the first block at or after `cutoff + latency` and
   `tape.state_at(stamp)`, the **market-only** reconstructed state at that block;
   `carry()` is applied (a no-op here, there is no position yet) and
   `curve.quote_curve_buy(state, 10**16)` prices the leg.
2. On the fill, `open_long` stores
   `self.own_delta[tape.curve] = {"quote": quote.net_into_curve, "tokens": quote.tokens_out}`.
3. At the horizon, `close` → `plan_sell(tape, horizon_ts, …)` → `fill_at(...)` again
   returns the **market-only** state at the exit block, and then
   `self.carry(tape, at["state"])` returns that state with `+quote` added to
   `quote_reserve` **and** to `real_quote_reserve` and `−tokens` taken off
   `token_reserve` and `sellable_tokens`. `curve.quote_curve_sell` is asked on that.
   The tape itself is never modified — `tests/d10/test_paper.py::test_the_paper_position_does_not_change_the_recorded_market`.

**What the 13,704-trade reproduction does and does not validate.** `MANIFEST.json` of
`d10-replay-v1` records `settled_trade_replay`: 8,891 of 8,891 `CurveBuy` and 4,813
of 4,813 `CurveSell` re-priced exactly from the reconstructed pre-trade state, with
895 of 895 snipe-taxed buys reproducing the charged wei. Every one of those 13,704
re-quotes prices **a trade that actually happened, on the market-only state that
actually preceded it**. It validates the formula — `getAmountOut`, the fee split, the
creator tax, the fourteen-halvings snipe decay — and it validates the reconstruction
that produces the pre-trade state. It does **not** validate the counterfactual
sequence, and there is a direct demonstration of the gap: advancing
`CurveReconstruction` from the *post-buy* state through the same external events
raises `SIM_SELL_REPLAY_MISMATCH` on the first external sell, because those events
reconcile against the reserves the chain actually had and not against reserves
carrying a position that was never there. That is precisely why `paper.py` advances
the market-only tape and applies `carry` at quote time instead of injecting the
position into the tape.

**What does support the counterfactual, and what stays an assumption.**

* Supported by fixtures and tests: `tests/d10/test_curve.py::test_the_donor_fixture_is_reproduced_wei_for_wei`

  behind**, checked field for field including `nextState`'s four reserves and
  `lossBeforeGas`; `tests/d10/test_paper.py::test_the_positions_own_impact_is_carried_into_its_exit`
  — the carried state has a strictly higher quote reserve and a strictly lower token
  reserve than the plain one; `::test_a_quiet_round_trip_loses_exactly_the_costs` —
  with no external flow the round trip returns exactly what it put in, so the whole
  net is the cost model; `::test_the_paper_position_does_not_change_the_recorded_market`
  — the tape's points are byte-identical before and after an entry.
* Assumptions, declared and unrelieved: **no reaction of other traders to our order** —
  the 19 external trades of episode 1 are the trades that happened without us, and
  nothing models how they would have differed; **no MEV** — no reordering, no
  sandwich, no priority-fee competition, and the fill is placed at the first block at
  or after `cutoff + 2 s` with no contention; **the approval leg is a 60,000-gas
  assumption** at the same gas price, not a receipt (`config.json`,
  `gas.approval_is_an_assumption`), while the buy and sell gas are the donor's
  calibration medians over 40 + 40 receipts and not this run's; and the fill block

### B.4 How many outcomes reached maximum reinforcement intensity

**The rule, quoted from the code.** `flytrade/execution.py`:

```
REINFORCE_FULL_SCALE = 0.01   # net return on notional that maps to a full-strength dopamine event
REINFORCE_CAP = 1.0           # hard cap on the dopamine amount, whatever the outcome

r        = net_pnl / notional
valence  = +1 if r > 0 else -1 if r < 0 else 0   (0 delivers nothing)
amount   = min(|r| / REINFORCE_FULL_SCALE, REINFORCE_CAP)
```

`PonsPaperExecution` inherits `reinforcement()` unchanged and adds no constant
(`config.json`, `reinforcement.mapping`). `experiments/historical/run.py::_reinforce`
then calls `Journal.settle(episode_id, payload, valence, amount)`, which routes the
pair into `CreditAssigner.settle` → `_apply_normalised`: the k = 8 stored traces each
produce a proposed gain delta against the one pre-update state `g0` through
`MushroomBody.proposed_gain_delta`, the deltas are averaged, `g0 + mean` is clipped
into `[floor, 1.0]` once, and that is the single update. `amount` enters only as the
multiplier in `gain *= (1 − lr · amount · trace)`.

**Every `LEARNING` record of the three logs.** There are eight: seven in
`d10-001/learning` and one in `d10-live-001`. `d10-001/frozen_reference` wrote **zero**
by construction — FROZEN settles through `Journal.settle_frozen`, which writes an
`OUTCOME`, counts `SETTLED_FROZEN` and calls no reinforcement at all — so its eight
settlements have a derivable pre-clip amount and no applied amount.

## E. What is not computable from the logs

1. **The pending live episode's outcome.** Episode 72000315 (`0xbf4c964f…`, entry
   block 60,924,110, horizon 1789198562) was `PENDING_CONFIRMATION` at the stop and
   then `END_OF_DATA`; **no exit leg was ever quoted**, so its gross, fees, impact,
   net, valence and amount do not exist in the logs. Its horizon timestamp does lie
   inside the run's own chain store, so an exit could be arithmetically quoted now —
   that is deliberately not done here, because it would be a settlement the
   confirmation rule refused.
2. **Per-replicate scores for the candidates that were not chosen.** The `DECISION`
   record carries `replicate_scores` and `replicate_statuses` for the chosen candidate
   only; the `ROUND` record carries each candidate's aggregate `score`, seed,
   `silent_replicates` and eligibility counts, but not its eight per-replicate
   scores. Which replicate drove a non-chosen candidate's score is therefore not
   recoverable.
3. **Realised gas.** No transaction was ever sent. The buy and sell gas are the
   donor's calibration medians over 40 + 40 receipts and the approval leg is a
   60,000-gas assumption; what these specific paper trades would have paid is not a
   measurable quantity anywhere in this wave.
4. **Other traders' reaction, and MEV.** Whether the 19 external trades of episode 1 —
   or the zero external trades of the other fifteen entries — would have differed had
   the order been real is outside the data by construction, as is any reordering,
   sandwich or priority-fee effect.
5. **The market-only exit quote for twelve of the sixteen settled episodes** (B.3):
   the counterfactual raises `QUOTE_EXCEEDS_REAL_RESERVE`, so there is no number to
   compare against, only the shortfall.
6. **Lifetimes beyond each collection's own edge.** `d10-replay-v1` stops at 62 s per
   token and cannot answer anything past it. In `d10-backfill-v1` the last trade of 5
   of 555 followed tokens falls within 60 s of the window end, and in the live store 6
   of 612 do; for those the "time to the last trade" is itself a lower bound.
7. **Per-method request counts by scope.** The ledger records methods for the wave and
   totals for each run id, but not the cross-product. `d10-live-000` and
   `d10-live-001` carry their own method splits in their summaries; `d10-verify` and
   `d10-backfill` do not have a complete one anywhere (see C.2), so the verification's
   and the collection's method breakdowns cannot both be reconstructed from the
   ledger alone.
8. **Fill and reference prices at full precision are absent from the records.**
   `Fill.as_dict` rounds them to eight decimals and a curve price near 1.7e-09 is
   stored as `0.0`; every price in section A was re-derived from the reconstructed
   state, not read from the log.
9. **Wall-clock time per decision.** `DECISION.brain_ms` is brain time (100 steps ×
   20 ms), not elapsed time; the per-round wall clock is not journalled, only the
   branch total.
10. **Anything about predictive skill.** Conclusion 4 of both reports reads *not
    tested* by construction: there is no label, no cohort split, no holdout and no
    control policy in D10, so no quantity in this file — the number of episodes, their
    sign, their sum, the decision mix or its movement — is evidence for or against it.
